Privacy & Cookies

This notice explains how M&M Villas Vourvourou handles personal data when you browse the website, check availability or send a direct booking request.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is M&M Villas Vourvourou, Vourvourou 630 78, Halkidiki, Greece. You can contact the property through the contact options shown on the website, including WhatsApp.

2. Data used by the website

  • Necessary preferences: the selected language and your analytics choice may be stored locally on your device.
  • Availability: the public calendar receives only unit codes and unavailable dates from the property calendar. Guest names, notes and payment information are not exposed to the public website.
  • Optional analytics: only after you accept analytics, the site may use a random visitor ID and session ID and record page path, language, device category, referrer, campaign parameters and website interaction events. The free-text booking message is not sent to analytics.
  • Booking request: dates, guest numbers and your message are prepared in your browser and sent to WhatsApp when you choose to continue there.

3. Why we use the data

We use data to operate the website, show availability, respond to booking enquiries, maintain security, and—only with your consent—understand website usage and improve the service.

4. Legal bases

Necessary website operations are processed to provide the service you request and for legitimate operational/security purposes where applicable. Booking enquiry data is processed to take steps at your request before a possible accommodation contract. Optional analytics is activated only after your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through “Cookie settings”.

5. Service providers and external links

Supabase is used for the website’s availability and analytics backend. If you open WhatsApp or Google Maps, you leave this website and those providers process data under their own privacy terms.

6. Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, for booking administration, security, accounting or legal obligations. Optional analytics data is periodically reviewed and should be deleted or aggregated when no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

8. Cookies and local storage

Necessary storage is used for language and privacy preferences. Optional first-party analytics storage is disabled by default and is activated only after a positive choice. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from browsing the website or sending a booking request.

9. Exterior security camera

The property states that an exterior security camera covers the parking area. It is used for property and guest security, not for website analytics. Any footage should be limited to the necessary area and retained only for the period needed for security or incident handling in accordance with applicable law.
