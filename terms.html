Booking Terms

These terms describe how direct booking requests made through mmvillasvourvourou.com are handled.

1. A website request is not yet a confirmed booking

Submitting the website form opens a WhatsApp conversation with the requested dates and guest information. A reservation becomes confirmed only when the owner explicitly confirms availability and any required payment or deposit arrangements.

2. Availability

The website calendar is connected to the property availability system and is designed to prevent selection of dates currently unavailable for the relevant group size. Booking.com and Airbnb calendars are also synchronised through iCal, which may update at intervals rather than instantly. Final availability is therefore confirmed by the owner before a reservation is accepted.

3. Accommodation setup

  • Main Villa: up to 8 guests.
  • Garden Villa: up to 4 guests where offered separately.
  • Entire Estate: up to 12 guests across both villas.

The recommended setup shown on the website is guidance; the final accommodation arrangement is confirmed with the owner.

4. Check-in and check-out

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, standard check-in begins at 13:00 and check-out is by 11:00.

5. Price, deposit, payment and cancellation

The final price, any deposit, payment schedule and cancellation conditions are communicated before confirmation. A booking should not be considered final until those conditions are accepted by both guest and owner.

6. Guest information

Guests should provide accurate dates, party size and relevant requirements. Special requests are subject to confirmation and availability.

7. External platforms

If a reservation is made through Booking.com or Airbnb, the applicable platform terms and the confirmed property conditions for that reservation apply.

8. Website information

We aim to keep descriptions, photographs, facilities and availability accurate. Temporary operational changes may occur. Material details that affect a booking should be confirmed before the reservation is finalised.

9. Privacy

Personal data and analytics are handled as described in the Privacy & Cookies notice.

10. Applicable rights

These terms do not limit any mandatory consumer rights that apply under Greek or European Union law.

For a specific reservation, the written confirmation agreed with the owner prevails for dates, price, payment and cancellation conditions.
